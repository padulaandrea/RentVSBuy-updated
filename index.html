Tax estimate optional

A tax deduction does not reimburse mortgage interest or property tax dollar-for-dollar. It reduces taxable income. The model compares what you would deduct as a renter with what you would deduct as an owner, then estimates the tax savings from the difference.

How to choose these tax inputs

Use your latest tax return as the baseline. The federal defaults are planning values for tax year 2026. California values use the latest published 2025 state figures and should be updated when newer state forms are available.

Conservative Turn the estimator off. This assumes no ownership-related tax savings.
Generic Use federal only when the state is unknown or you want a portable baseline.
California Use federal + CA when the home and tax residency are in California.
Input What to enter Your current starting point
Federal marginal rate The rate applying to the last dollars of taxable income, not your effective tax rate. 32% based on your latest taxable income.
Federal SALT baseline State/local taxes you already pay while renting. Exclude the prospective home's property tax. $41,094 from your latest Schedule A.
Other federal itemized deductions Deductions that remain in both scenarios, excluding mortgage interest and the prospective property tax. $0 for 2026 if the only contribution remains $785; the new federal 0.5%-of-AGI charitable floor would eliminate it at your income.
CA marginal rate Your California marginal income-tax rate. 9.3% based on your latest CA taxable income.
CA other itemized deductions California-allowed deductions present in both scenarios. Do not include CA income tax because California does not allow a deduction for its own income tax. $1,676 = $891 personal-property tax + $785 charity.
Manual additional benefit Only a separate incremental amount from tax software or a professional that is not already modeled. $0 to avoid double counting.

The California estimator is intentionally simplified. If your California AGI rises above the itemized-deduction limitation threshold, use tax software for a final number. The app will warn you when the entered AGI is above that threshold.

How the tax estimate works

The estimator compares the buyer's deduction with the renter's deduction.

Important: This is tax savings, not recovered interest. Mortgage principal, homeowners insurance, maintenance, and HOA are not treated as homeowner itemized deductions in this simplified federal/California model.

The model credits the estimated benefit to the buyer portfolio at each year-end. It reduces net housing cost, unrecoverable cost, and the economic ownership premium, but it does not reduce gross mortgage or housing cash outflow.

The entered tax rules are reused for every simulated year. Revisit them when tax law changes, especially for long holding periods.

How the model treats money

  • Investment and home appreciation compound monthly.
  • Monthly surplus or deficit is added to each scenario's investment portfolio.
  • Mortgage principal builds equity; mortgage interest is unrecoverable.
  • Buyer net worth subtracts the remaining mortgage, selling costs, and estimated gain tax.
  • Property tax grows independently from market appreciation.
  • Investment return should be entered after expected tax drag and fees.

This calculator is for scenario planning. Confirm tax, insurance, financing, and property-specific costs with qualified professionals before making a purchase decision.
